# Quick Start
*New to Crystal Quest? Start here. This page gets you playing your first game. The full Rules cover every detail.*

*Rather learn by playing? Try [Learn to Play](#learn): a few quick screens, then a coached game against the AI. You can keep the deck afterwards.*

## 1. The Goal

You lead a party of three Heroes. **Defeat all of your opponents’ Heroes to win.** A Hero is defeated when its HP drops to 0. The last player with a Hero still standing wins. You also lose if you have to draw a card when your library is empty.

If a card ever disagrees with the rules, the card wins.

## 2. What You Need

- **3 Heroes** and a deck of **at least 50 cards** (up to 3 copies of a card, 1 of each Legendary).
- A six-sided die for each Hero to track its level.
- Counters for damage and status effects, and something to track your **Aura** and **Gold**.

Build your deck in the **Deck Builder**: pick three Heroes, then add cards of their classes and Generic cards. You can practice against the AI in the **Play** tab, or take a coached first game in [Learn to Play](#learn).

## 3. Build Your Party

- Pick three Heroes and put them in a row. Left to right they are your **Lead**, **Center** and **Closer**. That order decides when each one acts.
- A Hero can only play **cards of its own class** or **Generic** cards. So your three Heroes’ classes decide what goes in your deck.
- Each Hero has four stats: **STR** (attack damage), **DEF** (reduces damage taken), **Aura** (adds to your Aura each round) and **HP** (health).

## 4. Set Up

1. Place your three Heroes in party order. Every Hero starts at Level 0.
2. Shuffle your deck. This is your library.
3. Draw 4 cards. You may mulligan once (shuffle them back and draw 4 new cards).
4. Roll dice. The highest roll starts with **initiative** and acts first.

## 5. Your Two Resources

| Resource | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Aura** | Spent to play cards and abilities. At Dawn it refills to the total Aura of your living Heroes. Unspent Aura is lost at Dusk. |
| **Gold** | Earned at Dusk: 1 Gold per living Hero. It carries over, and you spend it in Town. |

## 6. A Round

Every round has four phases, and all players go through them together.

| Phase | What you do |
|---|---|
| **1. Dawn** | Ready your Heroes, refill your Aura, lose Health Points from Burn, Poison and Wound counters, then draw a card. |
| **2. Battle** | Take turns acting, one Hero at a time, in party order: Lead, then Center, then Closer. Keep going until everyone passes. |
| **3. Dusk** | Lose unspent Aura, collect Gold, discard down to 5 cards, and pass initiative. |
| **4. Town** | Spend Gold: heal at the Inn, draw at the Shop, upgrade gear at the Blacksmith, or resurrect a Hero at the Church. |

## 7. What a Hero Can Do

When it’s your turn to act, the Hero in the marked position takes **one action**:

- **Play a card** of its class (or a Generic card) by paying its Aura cost.
- **Attack** any target. Damage is your STR minus the target’s DEF. Attacking exhausts the Hero.
- **Activate an ability** written as “cost: effect” on a Hero, item or piece of equipment.
- **Level Up**: exhaust the Hero, raise its level by 1 and draw a card. At Levels 2, 4 and 6 it gains +1 STR, DEF or Aura.
- **Equip** a piece of equipment by paying its Aura cost.
- **Step Back** to act last this round.

An **exhausted** Hero (turned sideways) can’t act again until the next Dawn. When you have nothing more to do, say **“I pass.”**

## 8. Keywords to Know

Cards explain their keywords in *(italic reminder text)*. A few you’ll see a lot:

- **Piercing X**: its damage ignores X of the target’s DEF, and at least X always gets through (no number: ignores all of it).
- **Combo**: after the card resolves, reveal your top card. If it’s a Combo card, you may play it.
- **Stun**: the target can’t play cards or activate abilities.
- **Burn, Poison, Wound**: the target loses 1 Health Point at Dawn.
- **Hidden** cards are set face down and spring when their trigger happens.

## 9. Tips for Your First Game

- **Spend your Aura every round.** Whatever you don’t use is lost at Dusk.
- **Watch your position.** Your Lead acts first, so give it your fastest plays.
- **Level up early** when you have nothing better to do. It draws a card and makes your Hero stronger.
- **Protect your weakest Hero.** Every Hero alive earns Aura and Gold.
- **Save Gold for Town.** The Inn and the Church can turn a close game around.
