# Set Two Rules Supplement
*Every rule and term that exists only in Set Two. It adds to the full Crystal Quest Rules; nothing here is defined in the main rules, so players without Set Two access never see it.*

*Playtest · Nov 12, 2026*

## About This Supplement

- **Who sees it:** only accounts with Set Two access on Deck Forge.
- **How it combines:** use the main rules for everything not covered here. If this supplement and the main rules disagree, this supplement wins in games that include Set Two cards.
- **Status:** playtest only. Every rule and card here can change between updates.

## Set Two Rule Changes

These changes apply only in games that include Set Two cards. They are queued and not yet in the main rules.

1. **Poison is Health Point loss.** A Poison counter makes its target lose Health Points instead of dealing damage. It does not trigger effects that care about damage, such as Descale.
2. **Descale needs damage dealt.** Descale triggers whenever damage is dealt to the Hero, even if its Defense prevents all of that damage.
3. **Revenants and Health Point loss.** Impervious stops damage and destroy effects, but a Revenant still dies when it loses all its Health Points, including from Poison.

## Necromancer

Necromancers raise an army of Revenants, then choose between growing it and cashing it in for Aura, cards or bigger summons. They also use their discard pile as a resource and Mill cards to fuel it.

- **Heroes:** 0 Strength, 1 Defense, 1 Aura, 16 Health Points.
- **Revenants:** every Revenant is a 1/1 with Impervious. Summoning one always exhausts the Hero; each card says so.
- **Keywords:** Mourn, Exhume, Impervious, Mill.

## Dragonborn

Dragonborn Heroes start with high Defense that wears down as they are hit. Opponents slug through their scales.

- **Keyword:** Descale.
- **Status:** in progress; Heroes and cards are still being designed.

## Artificer

Artificers make things. They play two-sided Recipe cards, spend ingredients from the party's inventory, and flip the Recipe to reveal what they made: Constructs, items or weapons for the party.

- **Recipe:** a two-sided card type. The front lists its ingredients and Aura cost. The back shows what it makes.
- **Craft:** play this only if its ingredients are in your inventory. Pay its Aura cost, sacrifice the ingredients, then flip the card. A made item or weapon goes into your inventory. A made summon enters play, and the Recipe then goes to your discard pile.
- **Ingredients** always come from your inventory unless a card says otherwise. "Any Metal" means any card with the Metal subtype. "Any Cloak" means any equipment card with Cloak in its name.
- **Metal:** a generic item subtype (Copper, Iron, Silver, Platinum and Mithril Ingots). Each Metal has its own use, so any class can play them; Artificers also use them as ingredients.
- **Quicksilver:** a special Artificer material (2 Aura item). It does nothing on its own, but it can be used as any ingredient.
- **Stall** (Pocket Watch): set the card being played aside with 2 stall counters on it. Each time that player takes an action, remove a stall counter. When the last one is removed, they may play the card without paying its Aura cost. The Aura they already paid is not refunded.
- **Protection counters** (Warded Cloak): whenever a Hero in your party would be dealt damage, prevent that damage and remove a protection counter instead. With no counters left, nothing is prevented.
- **Made weapons** are equipped by the normal rules: a Hero equips one from your inventory to itself by paying the weapon's Aura cost.

## Glossary

Every term new in Set Two, alphabetical.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Abomination | A 5/5 summon token made by From Darkness. It enters with Stealth and ready. It does not have Impervious. |
| Construct | A summon made by an Artificer Recipe. |
| Craft | See Artificer. |
| Descale | Whenever damage is dealt to this Hero, it permanently loses 1 Defense. Triggers even if Defense prevents all the damage. |
| Exhume | If this card was moved from your library to your discard pile this turn, you may play it from your discard pile. You still pay its costs. |
| Impervious | This can't be dealt damage or destroyed. It still dies if it loses all its Health Points. Sacrificing it is allowed. |
| Metal | See Artificer. |
| Mill X | Move the top X cards of a library to its discard pile. |
| Mourn | If you've surrendered a soul this game, you may play this card without paying its Aura cost. You still pay all other costs. Mourn is printed first on the card. |
| Quicksilver | See Artificer. |
| Recipe | See Artificer. |
| Remains | An item token made by Cursed Urn. Sacrifice it to gain 1 Aura, spent only on Summon cards. |
| Revenant | A 1/1 summon token with Impervious, made by Necromancer cards. Summoning one always exhausts the Hero. |
| Stall | See Artificer. |
