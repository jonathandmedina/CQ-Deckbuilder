# Trials Module Supplement
*Co-op boss Trials for Crystal Quest. These rules add to the full rules and only apply in a Trial. If this supplement disagrees with the full rules during a Trial, this supplement wins.*

*Version 1.1 · Nov 13, 2026 · Module 1: Trial 1, the Rock Mammoth*

## 1. What a Trial Is

In a **Trial**, every player is on the same team against a **boss** that runs itself from its own deck. Clear the boss’s spawns, wear it down, and bring it to 0 Health Points. Module 1 has five Trials of rising difficulty; your group’s result is how far you got.

## 2. What You Need

- Each player brings a **party of 2 Heroes** and a deck of **at least 50 cards** (up to 3 copies of a card, 1 of each Legendary).
- The boss card, its 20-card boss deck, and its spawn tokens.
- Counters for Stone Plates and Rage.

## 3. Setting Up

1. Each party places its Heroes in **Lead** and **Closer**. A Hero that Steps Back still acts at Last.
2. Each player shuffles and draws as normal. A player who won loot adds it to their opening hand after drawing, before mulligans (see Loot). Each party rolls a die; the highest roll starts with initiative (reroll ties).
3. Set the boss’s Health Points and Defense for the number of parties (P). The **Rock Mammoth** has 25 × P Health Points and starts with P Stone Plates.
4. The boss spawns **one spawn against each party**.
5. Shuffle the boss deck. If it ever runs out, shuffle its discard pile back in; the boss never loses for an empty deck.

## 4. The Round

Dawn, Battle, Dusk and Town work as in the full rules, with these changes:

1. **Dawn:** the boss and spawns ready and take status damage (Burned, Poisoned, Wounded) like anything else.
2. **Battle:** each party acts with its Lead, in initiative order. That is a **sweep**.
3. **Boss flip:** after every sweep, flip the top card of the boss deck and do what it says. Then each spawn attacks.
4. Sweeps continue (Lead, Closer, Last) with a boss flip after each, until every party passes.
5. **Dusk and Town:** each party collects its own Gold and visits Town on its own, in initiative order. Initiative passes among the parties; the boss never has it.

## 5. Winning and Losing

- **Win:** the boss reaches 0 Health Points.
- **Lose:** every party is wiped out at the same time. Conceding also ends the Trial as a loss for the whole group.
- A wiped party sits out the rest of the Trial but can still Surrender souls to help its allies. It skips Town and doesn’t draw at Dawn. A party that must draw from an empty library is wiped out (its Heroes die); the Trial goes on.

## 6. The Boss and Your Cards

- **The boss counts as an enemy Hero** for all card text, so cards that hit “enemy Heroes” hit it. (This is temporary; cards will be reworded so it isn’t needed.)
- If the boss is Stunned or exhausted, it **skips its next flip**.
- The boss can’t be stolen from or taken control of. It has no Gold, Aura, hand or level. Fatigue and Confuse do nothing to it. Each Stun or exhaust makes it skip only one flip, and it readies right away.
- **Spawns** are enemy summons. “All enemies” hits them; “each enemy Hero” doesn’t.
- **Allies** are every Hero on the player team, including other players’ Heroes. “Your” still means only your own party: your Gold, Aura, hand, deck and Town visit stay your own.
- “Each opponent” and “each other player” mean the boss side. Effects for “all parties” skip the boss.

## 7. Spawns

- **Your spawns first.** While a spawn placed against your party is alive, your party can’t attack or target spawns placed against other parties. You can always attack the boss. Effects that hit all enemies still hit every spawn.
- **Intelligence Level 1** (Trials 1 to 3): after each boss flip, a spawn attacks the Hero with the most Health Points in the party it was spawned against. Ties go to the Hero with the least Defense, then Lead before Closer, then initiative order. If that party is wiped out, it attacks the next party clockwise. A Stunned spawn doesn’t attack.

## 8. Boss Cards

Every boss card names its **target** and its **effect**. If more than one Hero or party fits, use the spawn tie-break: least Defense, then Lead before Closer, then initiative order. Damage on boss cards is before Defense. If no party has damaged the boss this round, Grudge Stomp uses the tie-break among the parties’ Leads; a dead Lead is replaced by its Closer.

## 9. Trial 1: The Rock Mammoth

The guardian of the mountain the parties must cross. It won’t let them pass safely.

- **Health Points:** 25 per party. **Defense:** 1 for each Stone Plate it still has.
- **Stone Plates:** it starts with 1 per party. Whenever a Golem is destroyed, break a Stone Plate (−1 Defense for the rest of the Trial) and put a **Rage** counter on the Rock Mammoth. New Golems don’t repair plates.
- **Raging Stampede:** once the Rock Mammoth has Rage counters equal to the number of parties plus 1, if it would flip a card, instead it does Raging Stampede. Don’t flip or reveal the card. Raging Stampede deals 3 damage to every player Hero, then remove all Rage counters, and the Rock Mammoth is **Winded** (0 Defense) until its next flip. Raging Stampede counts as its flip, so it also ends Stone Skin.
- **Golem** (spawn): 1 Strength, 0 Defense, 4 Health Points.
- **Getting past its Defense:** status damage, Piercing and “lose Health Points” effects work on it as usual, and on-hit statuses land even when its Defense stops all the damage.

## 10. Loot

- When a Trial is won, reveal one loot card per party, plus one, from that Trial’s loot pool. The group gives one to each party; the extra goes back.
- All loot is **Legendary, Generic** gear that is fair in a regular game too.
- Loot starts in your **opening hand** in your next Trial. You still pay its Aura cost to equip it. It goes into your hand after the opening draw and before mulligans, it isn’t one of your 50 cards, and it stays in your hand if you mulligan.
- Loot is the only thing you keep between Trials.

Trial 1 loot: Rockbreaker Maul, Mammoth Tusk, Crag Helm, Stampede Boots, Stoneplate Mantle, Heart of the Mountain. See them in Cards under **Type: Trials module**.

## 11. Between Trials

**Standard mode:** a full reset between Trials. Decks reshuffle, Heroes return at full Health Points and Level 0, and gear and Gold are cleared. Only loot carries over, so a group can start at the Trial it last reached.

## 12. Glossary

- **Trial:** one co-op boss fight in the Trials module.
- **Boss:** the enemy the players fight together; it runs itself from its boss deck.
- **Boss flip:** after each sweep, the top card of the boss deck is flipped and resolved.
- **Spawn:** an enemy summon the boss places against a party (the Rock Mammoth’s spawn is the Golem).
- **Stone Plate:** the Rock Mammoth’s armor; each gives +1 Defense and breaks when a Golem is destroyed.
- **Rage:** counters the Rock Mammoth gets when Golems are destroyed; enough of them trigger Raging Stampede.
- **Raging Stampede:** the Rock Mammoth’s charge, done instead of a boss flip.
- **Winded:** the Rock Mammoth has 0 Defense until its next flip.
- **Loot:** Legendary gear won by clearing a Trial.
- **Intelligence Level:** how smart spawns are when they choose targets; Trials 1 to 3 use Level 1.
